<hr data-start="380" data-end="383">

<h2 data-start="385" data-end="481">What kind of game is <em data-start="409" data-end="419">Sakigake</em>? Why it caught the attention of Brave Frontier Heroes players</h2>
<p data-start="483" data-end="628">First off, the reason this game grabbed my attention—<br data-start="536" data-end="539">is because it’s the latest title from DJT, the team behind <em data-start="598" data-end="627">Brave Frontier Heroes (BFH)</em>.</p>
<p data-start="630" data-end="923"><strong data-start="630" data-end="679">“Three Kingdoms × NFT × Web3 × Strategy Game”</strong><br data-start="679" data-end="682">These keywords alone set off all the alert bells in my gamer brain.<br data-start="749" data-end="752">To top it off, I got lucky and was selected for the Closed Beta via a Champions Tactics ranking campaign!<br data-start="857" data-end="860">I’ve been playing thoroughly since before the official release.</p>

<hr data-start="925" data-end="928">

<h2 data-start="930" data-end="999">First Impressions: Subtle visuals—but strangely addicting strategy</h2>
<p data-start="1001" data-end="1187">To be honest, my first impression was that the presentation was rather modest.<br data-start="1079" data-end="1082">Skill effects are simple, and the UI is minimal.<br data-start="1130" data-end="1133">But as I kept playing, I realized something important:</p>
<p data-start="1189" data-end="1238"><strong data-start="1189" data-end="1238">“…This game’s mind games run seriously deep…”</strong></p>
<p data-start="1240" data-end="1450">At first glance, <em data-start="1257" data-end="1267">Sakigake</em> looks like a simple turn-based battle game.<br data-start="1311" data-end="1314">But in reality, it demands multi-layered strategic thinking—<br data-start="1374" data-end="1377">such as troop type matchups, target priority, and turn order predictions.</p>
<p data-start="1452" data-end="1800">It’s not a flashy game where you win by spamming skills.<br data-start="1508" data-end="1511">Rather, it’s about anticipating unit movement, placement, and priority of actions.<br data-start="1593" data-end="1596">Imagine a hybrid between <strong data-start="1621" data-end="1679">chess, mahjong, and the Romance of the Three Kingdoms.</strong><br data-start="1679" data-end="1682">Each match feels weighty and meaningful—it’s the kind of game that makes you want to pour your soul into every battle.</p>

<hr data-start="1802" data-end="1805">

<h2 data-start="1807" data-end="1881">Real talk as a working adult: One match a day is enough—and that’s okay</h2>
<p data-start="1883" data-end="2075">As a full-time working adult, I honestly don’t have hours to spend on games every day.<br data-start="1969" data-end="1972">But that’s what’s great about <em data-start="2002" data-end="2012">Sakigake</em>: even <strong data-start="2019" data-end="2039">one battle a day</strong> gives you a true sense of progress.</p>
<p data-start="2077" data-end="2279">I log in for a bit daily, tweak my setup, play one match, and then go,<br data-start="2147" data-end="2150">“Alright… I won today!” with a quiet sense of satisfaction.<br data-start="2209" data-end="2212">For slow-paced strategy gamers like me, it’s the perfect companion.</p>

<hr data-start="2281" data-end="2284">

<h2 data-start="2286" data-end="2349">My starter deck and lucky draw: Thank you, Awakened Cao Cao!</h2>
<p data-start="2351" data-end="2443">Early on, I spent about 10,000 yen on gacha,<br data-start="2395" data-end="2398">and thankfully I pulled <strong data-start="2422" data-end="2442">Awakened Cao Cao</strong>!</p>
<p data-start="2445" data-end="2708">He’s incredibly versatile—<br data-start="2471" data-end="2474">with well-rounded stats, skills, and troop type,<br data-start="2522" data-end="2525">he became my anchor unit and the basis for my entire lineup.<br data-start="2585" data-end="2588">Thanks to him, even with my slow play pace as a working adult,<br data-start="2650" data-end="2653">I was able to build a team that could win consistently.</p>

<hr data-start="2710" data-end="2713">

<h2 data-start="2715" data-end="2800">About the Valor Points System this season: If your spirit breaks, blame the system</h2>
<p data-start="2802" data-end="2884">One word of caution—<br data-start="2822" data-end="2825">this season’s special <strong data-start="2847" data-end="2870">Valor Points system</strong> can be harsh.</p>
<p data-start="2886" data-end="3092">If you lose, your Valor Points drop dramatically,<br data-start="2935" data-end="2938">which can be really tough on working players’ morale.<br data-start="2991" data-end="2994">Seeing your hard-earned points disappear after a losing streak…<br data-start="3057" data-end="3060">honestly, it <em data-start="3073" data-end="3080">hurts</em> (tears up).</p>
<p data-start="3094" data-end="3160">I even stopped playing for about the last two weeks of the season.</p>
<p data-start="3162" data-end="3311">But! The devs have said that <strong data-start="3191" data-end="3241">this is a one-time system for this season only</strong>!<br data-start="3242" data-end="3245">We can expect more forgiving gameplay from the next season onward.</p>

<hr data-start="3313" data-end="3316">

<h2 data-start="3318" data-end="3379">About controls: Just use the PC browser version, seriously</h2>
<p data-start="3381" data-end="3423">This is important, so I’ll say it clearly:</p>
<p data-start="3425" data-end="3492"><strong data-start="3425" data-end="3492">Don’t use the smartphone version—play on PC browser. Seriously.</strong></p>
<p data-start="3494" data-end="3620">From the fine UI details to scroll responsiveness and precise target selection,<br data-start="3573" data-end="3576">PC offers a far more comfortable experience.</p>
<p data-start="3622" data-end="3715">I lost multiple matches due to misclicks and connection drops when using my phone.<br data-start="3704" data-end="3707">I cried.</p>

<hr data-start="3717" data-end="3720">

<h2 data-start="3722" data-end="3783">Favorite Generals: I vibe with the Cao Cao &amp; Sima Yi combo</h2>
<p data-start="3785" data-end="4026">Personally, my favorite generals are <strong data-start="3822" data-end="3845">Cao Cao and Sima Yi</strong>.<br data-start="3846" data-end="3849">Not only do they have great synergy in gameplay,<br data-start="3897" data-end="3900">but as a Romance of the Three Kingdoms nerd, I love the historical twist of<br data-start="3975" data-end="3978">“in the end, it’s Sima Yi who unifies the land.”</p>
<p data-start="4028" data-end="4073">Just having them in my deck boosts my morale.</p>

<hr data-start="4075" data-end="4078">

<h2 data-start="4080" data-end="4126">About fan works: Caution is advised for now</h2>
<p data-start="4128" data-end="4233">As someone who loves creating fan art and dot sprites,<br data-start="4182" data-end="4185">I’d love to support <em data-start="4205" data-end="4215">Sakigake</em> through my art...</p>
<p data-start="4235" data-end="4417">But currently, I haven’t found any official guidelines regarding <strong data-start="4300" data-end="4320">derivative works</strong> for this title.<br data-start="4336" data-end="4339">So for now, I recommend proceeding with caution and keeping creations private.</p>
<p data-start="4419" data-end="4514">I hope one day, just like in BFH, the community will be encouraged to share fan art officially!</p>
